The data required for the bounding area have been downloaded. The data surpasses 2GB so it cannot
be uploaded to github. Below are the websites where data was downloaded from. The .shp file inside
the "MLRA 42 B bounding area" folder was used to determine what the location for data downloaded.

Climate data: https://prism.oregonstate.edu/normals/
Elevation data: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/USGS_3DEP_10m_collection
Geology: https://ngmdb.usgs.gov/Prodesc/proddesc_119417.htm
reflectance data:https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/
SSURGO: contains a .csv that shows the dominant soil components within the bounding area and
the ecological site associated with that component.
MLRA 42 B bounding area: Contains the .shp file used to mask the data to our area of interest.
